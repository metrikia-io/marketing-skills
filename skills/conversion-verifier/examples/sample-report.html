What this means

Your three campaigns post the same ROAS. They are not the same campaigns. 1.94x, 2.00x and 1.97x on the dashboard. With view-through removed: 0.92x, 1.53x and 1.61x. That is a budget difference, not a measurement nuance.

Retargeting is the one that crosses the line. 53% of its claimed sales rest on people who never clicked. On the low case those 22,787 $ cost you money; on the high case they make it. Nothing in this report can settle that, and nothing will without a test.

Your measurement is not the problem. 684 claims on clicks against 641 store orders carrying a paid social referrer: the gap is small and points the right way. What is in question is not your tracking, it is what Meta agrees to count as a sale.

The decision

  • Cut retargeting on half the audience for two weeks. It is the only test that settles the 53%, and it puts 22,787 $ at stake either way.
  • Leave prospecting and Advantage+ alone. Even on the low case they sit at or just under break-even. There is nothing to arbitrate there.
  • Steer the week on blended MER, at 2.40x. It is above your 1.61x break-even and no attribution setting can inflate it.

What this analysis could not see

  • Ad account and store were assumed to share a timezone. Material on short windows, noise on long ones.

There is one question this report cannot answer: which order came from which ad. A standard export carries no order IDs, so it stops there. Metrikia answers, by reconciling orders one by one.
