The figures

What the numbers say

Meta reported 890 purchases against $68,380 of spend. The store recorded 1,139 orders over the same period, so nothing here is impossible on its face.

The finding is narrower and more useful: 306 of the 890 claimed purchases are view-through. The click-attributed portion holds up against the store's own referrer data. That leaves view-through as the whole contested zone.

On the revenue question, the account looks honest

Blended MER sits at 2.40x against a reported ROAS of 1.77x. MER uses no attribution model, so no attribution model can inflate it.

What this analysis could not see

  • Ad account and store were assumed to share a timezone. Material on short windows, noise on long ones.
